FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does it take?
The Starter package is delivered in 48–72 hours after I receive your content. Pro gets priority delivery, so it’s typically at the faster end of that window.
What do you need from me?
A short bio, your best work (links, images or descriptions), your contact details and any colors or sites you like. Don’t have everything? Send what you have and I’ll help shape the rest.
Can I make edits after delivery?
Both packages include 2 revision rounds before launch. After that, small updates are available at a friendly rate — just message me.
Where is the site hosted?
Your site is hosted live on GitHub Pages or Vercel — fast, reliable and free to keep running. I’ll set it up and share the link with you.
Do I need to buy a domain?
Not for Starter — you get a free shareable link. With Pro, I help you connect your own domain (like yourname.com) including DNS and SSL. You purchase the domain from a registrar of your choice; I’ll guide you.
What’s your refund policy?
If I haven’t started work, you get a full refund. If you’re not happy with the first preview and we can’t get it right within the included revisions, I’ll discuss a fair partial refund. Your satisfaction matters.
